# Fragmentation

Crashes as they are, for Kerbal Space Program 1: a craft that hits the ground breaks up from the parts that
hit it, slides on and comes apart as it goes, with its tanks leaking and going up along the way, and leaves
solid pieces of itself. It stands on [Keystone](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-keystone), and works with
[Volumetric Explosions](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-volumetric-explosions) where it is installed. The short
version is on the repository's front page.

* [How it works](How-it-works.md): what the game does with a crash and what this mod does instead; blows, the
  slide, what becomes of a part, the pieces, and what is said to the explosions mod
* [Research](Research.md): how other games break things, how real aircraft and rockets break up, and every
  number the mod uses with where it came from
* [What was tested](What-was-tested.md): the crashes it was tried on, what they looked like, and what they cost
* [Limits](Limits.md): what it does not do, or does only roughly
* [Building it yourself](Building.md)
