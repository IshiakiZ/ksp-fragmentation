# Fragmentation

Crashes as they are. A craft that hits the ground breaks up from the parts that hit it: tanks are crushed, torn open
as they slide, split, leak and go up, and the wreck digs a scar into the ground and leaves a trail of its own torn
pieces. Parts from other mods break up as the game's own do.

For Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x. **Needs [Keystone](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-keystone) 0.9 or later**, a separate download
([on SpaceDock](https://spacedock.info/mod/4665/Keystone)): this mod does not load without it.
[Volumetric Explosions](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-volumetric-explosions) ([on SpaceDock](https://spacedock.info/mod/4669/Volumetric%20Explosions))
is not needed, but the leaks, the fires and the smoke of every piece are drawn only with it.

## What it does

* **Parts tear:** a blow crushes the end that hit, and a part dragged along the ground has its underside torn away
  panel by panel.
* **A wreck digs in** on soil and scrapes on paving. It does not bound along the ground or roll on like a wheel.
* **The ground is what the world has there** (0.3.0): a wreck skates far on snow and sinks deep in it, ploughs to a stop in
  sand, slides on ice as on glass, scrapes and sparks on bare rock, and raises white powder, pale sand or grey dust as it goes.
* **Tanks split before they go up,** leaking and alight; what runs out stains the ground under them, and burns there
  as a pool where it is alight (0.3.0).
* **The pieces are the parts' own:** torn panels, rings out of torn tanks, engines' bells.
* **In orbit too:** a craft that clips another tears at what it hits.
* **On the sea** (0.3.0): a part striking the water takes a blow from the water it throws up, broadside nearly as on soil,
  nose first far less; the pieces sink slowly.
* **Heard** (0.3.0): grinding by the force of the slide on paving, steel or soil, each blow's crunch or clang, metal
  tearing.
* **Kept** (0.3.0): the scars, craters and stains on the ground and the torn parts are in the save, there again after a
  quickload.
* **Wrecks seen out** (0.4.0, a setting): pieces you are watching go on sliding and tumbling further off than the game keeps
  them moving, and a crash's worst frames are shorter.

## Install

Copy the `Fragmentation` folder from the download into your KSP's `GameData`, beside `Keystone`.

## Settings

In the Keystone window (the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Alt-K; Option-K on a Mac): how far a wreck
slides, how tough parts are, how many pieces there may be and how long they stay.

## Good to know

* A craft that touches down within its parts' crash tolerances is not hurt, as in the game. What water does is left
  to the game, but for the blow of the water on a part that strikes it.
* While a craft could hit something at speed, a mod that shows a part's crash tolerance will show a very large
  number: this mod is deciding what a blow does.
* A big crash costs about what the game's own crash with Volumetric Explosions costs.

## Works on

Mac: tried. Windows 11 (Direct3D 11): 0.2.2 tried; 0.4.0's crashes made cheaper there (measured and filmed) and its wrecks moving further
off tried (2026-10-24 and 25). Linux: never run.

**More:** [the long description](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-fragmentation/wiki/The-long-description) (everything in full, and what
was tried on each system) and the rest of the [wiki](https://github.com/IshiakiZ/ksp-fragmentation/wiki).
