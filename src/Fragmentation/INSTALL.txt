Fragmentation @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

Crashes as they are: what hits the ground is torn where it hits, its own skin crushed and
ripped open, not taken apart where its parts are joined. One that comes in at a slant digs
into soft ground, its underside torn away within metres; torn open, it scoops up the earth
and stops within tens of metres, its torn tanks leaking and going up, its fire and smoke going
with it. It leaves a scar of torn earth, craters, and real pieces of itself: torn panels,
rings and stringers, engines, shreds, rivets, clods and slabs of turf, every piece trailing a
little smoke. Parts from other mods are torn as the game's own are.

INSTALL
  Copy the three folders in this download (Keystone, VolumetricExplosions and
  Fragmentation) into your KSP's GameData folder, and start the game. Keystone is the base
  this mod stands on. Volumetric Explosions is not needed, but with it the explosions are
  its fire and smoke, and the leaks, the wreck's fire, the smoke of the pieces and the dust
  of a slide go into its air.
  To remove the mod, delete the Fragmentation folder.

USE
  Nothing to do: crash something. The settings (how far a wreck slides, how tough parts are,
  how many pieces there may be and for how long, leaks and fires, and switching it off) are
  in the Keystone window: the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Option-K on a Mac
  and Alt-K elsewhere. Then turn to Fragmentation.

GOOD TO KNOW
  While a craft is close enough to the ground or to another craft to hit it at speed, its
  parts' crash tolerance is raised (other mods that show it will show a very large number):
  the mod decides itself what a blow does. A craft that touches down within its parts'
  tolerances is not hurt, as in the game. What water does is left to the game. A torn part
  is drawn by this mod in place of the game's own drawing of it while the flight lasts.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac, and tried on Windows 11 (0.2.2, with Volumetric Explosions 0.6.3:
  rockets let fall by the pad and thrown at a wall). It has no shader of its own. The fire
  and smoke are Volumetric Explosions', drawn by that mod's shaders (for OpenGL on a Mac or
  Linux, built for Direct3D on Windows). Nobody has run it on Linux yet.
