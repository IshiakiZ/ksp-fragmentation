Fragmentation @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

Crashes as they are: a craft that hits the ground breaks up from the parts that hit it. One
that comes in at a slant slides and tumbles on along the ground, coming apart as it goes;
split tanks leak for a moment and then go up; and every part that is destroyed falls apart
into pieces of its own skin, which are solid and come to rest. Parts from other mods break
up as the game's own do.

INSTALL
  Copy the three folders in this download's GameData (Keystone, VolumetricExplosions and
  Fragmentation) into your KSP's GameData folder, and start the game. Keystone is the base
  this mod stands on. Volumetric Explosions is not needed, but with it the explosions are
  its fire and smoke, and leaks, burning pieces and the dust of a slide go into its air.
  To remove the mod, delete the Fragmentation folder.

USE
  Nothing to do: crash something. The settings (how far a wreck slides, how tough parts are,
  how many pieces there may be and for how long, leaks and fires, and switching it off) are
  in the Keystone window: the keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Option-K on a Mac
  and Alt-K elsewhere. Then turn to Fragmentation.

GOOD TO KNOW
  While a craft is close enough to the ground or to another craft to hit it at speed, its
  parts' crash tolerance is raised (other mods that show it will show a very large number):
  the mod decides itself what a blow does. A craft that touches down within its parts'
  tolerances is not hurt, as in the game. What water does is left to the game.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac. It has no shader of its own and nothing in it is particular to
  a Mac, but it has not been run on Windows or Linux. The fire and smoke of Volumetric
  Explosions are drawn by that mod's shader, which is made for OpenGL (a Mac, Linux); on
  Windows that mod falls back to its own simpler look.
